Work Done

Work Done

– The building was vacated on 01.01.2023 by the Muslim Social Uplift Society.
– All legal cases with the Muslim Social Uplift Society were mutually withdrawn.
– Elections to the AMU court were conducted for the term 01.01.2023 to 31.12.2025.
– Society re-registration was completed for the period 2009 to June 2024 and extended to 31.06.2029.
– A loan of Rs 4 lakh to BMCB was cleared in full.
– A children’s book on Sir Syed was prepared in English, Urdu, and Hindi.
– A coordination meeting was held with principals from 10 Aligarh schools.
– Scholarships totaling approximately Rs 8 lakh were awarded to about 300 candidates in the last session.
– A library section was renovated and established.
– House and shop tenants’ rental agreements were renegotiated; some overdue rents were recovered.
– A General Body meeting took place on 26.11.2023 after 30 years.
– Jhuggis and other encroachments outside the main gate were removed.
– Exterior and interior roads and the gate of Sultan Jahan Manzil were constructed or improved.
– 12A and 80G registrations were obtained under IT rules.
– A new Quran and Hadith center was introduced, with expenditures around Rs 9 lakh.
– Collaboration with Roshni Group led to Taraweeh and other Quranic programs.
– A new room with kitchen and bathroom was constructed; the main building’s back verandah was vacated.
– Essential equipment (tubewell, inverter, cooler, gas connection) and furniture (office furniture, steel almirah, computers, printer) were purchased.
– Ongoing renovations and restoration efforts continue under the Executive Committee’s authorization.
– Three air conditioners were donated for the Quranic center.
– Weekly IAS practice test exams are conducted on-site.
– Three books are ready for publication soon.
– Expert consultations were conducted for retrofitting a structurally weak room.
– A process to update addresses, WhatsApp contacts, and membership numbers for all members is underway.
